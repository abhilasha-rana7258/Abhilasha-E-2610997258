About College

Chitkara University is a leading multi-disciplinary private university located near Chandigarh, Punjab (with another major campus in Himachal Pradesh). Established in 2002 by the Chitkara Educational Trust, it offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs.
