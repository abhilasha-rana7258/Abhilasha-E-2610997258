College Terminology

Semester
A designated period of time into which the academic year is divided, typically lasting 15 to 16 weeks.
GPA
Grade Point Average, a standard numerical measure of a student's academic achievement calculated across all courses.
Prerequisite
A mandatory course or requirement that must be successfully completed before registering for a more advanced class.
Syllabus
An outline and summary of topics, assignments, grading policies, and expectations provided by the instructor at the beginning of a course.
Credit Hour
A unit of measure used to determine the total number of classroom hours per week a student spends taking a particular course.
