Taj Mahal(Agra)

Description: A stunning white marble mausoleum built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan on the south bank of the Yamuna river. Recognized globally as a symbol of eternal love, it is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the New Seven Wonders of the World.

Amer Fort (Jaipur)

Description: A majestic hilltop fort constructed from red sandstone and marble, known for its artistic Hindu architectural style, large ramparts, series of gates, and the breathtaking Sheesh Mahal (Mirror Palace).

Gateway of India (Mumbai)

Description: An iconic 20th-century arch monument built during the British Raj, overlooking the Arabian Sea at the waterfront in South Mumbai. It is one of the city's top landmarks and a major gathering spot.

Qutub Minar (New Delhi)

Description: A towering 73-meter-high victory minaret built in 1193, featuring intricate carvings and verses from the Quran. The complex also houses the mysterious Iron Pillar that has resisted rust for over 1,600 years.

Hawa Mahal (Jaipur)

Description: Known as the "Palace of Winds," this unique five-story pink sandstone structure features 953 small windows called jharokhas, designed to allow royal ladies to observe street life and festivals without being seen.
